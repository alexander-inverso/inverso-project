The vision

Bluntly, I want computers to simulate biology well enough that the solutions we need can be found rationally, with math, instead of stumbled upon in nature or in a wet lab.

Biology is deterministic on paper, but it definitely isn't in real life.  That's where the tension lies, and one of my goals is to find out where the limits are with supercomputers, AI, math and quantum computers, too, at some point. I know it will be a big deal.

As a kid I loved machines. What I want to build now is one that solves biotech problems for us.

A project in construction.
